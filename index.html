Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ride?
Enter your pickup and drop-off locations on the Home screen, tap Search Rides to compare real-time fares across Uber, Ola, Rapido, and Namma Yatri, then select your preferred ride to book.
How is the fare calculated?
Fares are calculated using real-time route distance, estimated travel duration, vehicle category (Bike, Auto, Cab), and live traffic conditions across each ride provider.
Can I cancel my ride?
Yes, you can cancel or modify your ride selection at any time from the booking confirmation screen before final dispatch.
Which payment methods are accepted?
UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, and cash are supported across all partner ride providers.
